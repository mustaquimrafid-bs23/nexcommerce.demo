# AI Design Guidelines & Voice — nexCommerce v1.0

## 1. Core Principles (§3, §18)

### Principle 01: Commerce First
nexCommerce is an **e-commerce platform first**. AI enhances discovery and shopping; it never replaces the commerce experience or obscures price, delivery, or return information.

### Principle 02: Intelligence in the Background
AI operates like an invisible personal shopping concierge.
- ✅ **Preferred**: *"Picked for your evening"* / *"Tell us what you need."*
- ❌ **Prohibited**: *"AI Recommendation Engine Result #402"* / *"Neural Network Output"*.

### Principle 03: Explain, Don't Impress
Recommendations should explain their usefulness in clear human terms:
- ✅ **Good**: **Why this fits:** *"Lightweight enough for an evening in Dhaka and aligns with your preference for minimal layering."*
- ❌ **Prohibited**: *"Match Probability: 98.4%"* / *"Machine Learning Confidence Score: High"*.

---

## 2. AI Color & Badge Standard (§18)
- **AI Accent**: Strictly uses the **Brand Pink-to-Crimson gradient** (`linear-gradient(135deg, #F13365, #E60C45)`).
- **Prohibition**: Never use generic purple or neon violet AI palettes.
- **Official AI Badges**:
  - `✦ AI MATCH`
  - `✦ AI PICK`
  - `✦ SELECTED FOR YOU`
  - `✦ WHY THIS FITS`

---

## 3. Natural Language Intent Discovery (§19)
- **Primary CTA**: *"Tell us what you need."*
- **Example Prompt**: *"Something for a winter evening in Dhaka"*
- **Parsed Context Chips**: `Understood as: Evening · Cool · Dhaka · Minimal`
- **Output**: Returns curated product matches accompanied by human-readable explanations.

---

## 4. Voice & Tone Spectrum (§25)
| Desired Tone | Examples | Prohibited Clichés |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Intelligent** | *"Shopping that understands what you mean."* | *"AI-POWERED AUTONOMOUS SHOPPING"* |
| **Human** | *"Find pieces worth discovering."* | *"NEURAL MATCH ALGORITHM V2"* |
| **Confident** | *"Designed for the distance."* | *"REVOLUTIONARY DISRUPTIVE SHOPPING"* |
| **Helpful** | *"Why this fits your schedule."* | *"MACHINE LEARNING AGENT ENGAGED"* |
