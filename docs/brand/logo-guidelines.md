# Logo Guidelines — nexCommerce v1.0

## 1. Overview & Official Mark
The official nexCommerce logo consists of the geometric wordmark `nexCommerce` featuring the signature magenta-to-crimson ribbon checkmark on the top right arm of the `x`, paired with the official strapline **`next generation e-commerce`**.

## 3. Logo Variations & Hierarchy (§4.2)
1. **Primary Lockup (Full Logo + Tagline)**:
   - Used on main desktop navigation headers, landing hero banners, and footers where space allows.
2. **Compact Lockup (Wordmark without Tagline)**:
   - Used in constrained spaces (mobile app bar, compact drawer headers, mini-cart header).
3. **Symbol / Favicon (The `x` Checkmark Motif)**:
   - Used for browser favicons, small app icons, and compact avatar marks.
4. **Monochrome Lockup**:
   - Approved pure white or single-color knockouts for high-contrast single-color printing.

---

## 4. Clear Space & Proportions (§4.3)
- **Minimum Clear Space**: The clear space surrounding the logo must be equal to or greater than the height of the lowercase `n` character on all 4 sides.
- **Aspect Ratio**: Always maintain original proportions (5.3:1 ratio).
- **Prohibited Modifications**:
  - ❌ Never stretch, squish, or compress horizontally or vertically.
  - ❌ Never rotate or tilt the logo mark.
  - ❌ Never recolor the wordmark with unauthorized hues.
  - ❌ Never add drop shadows, outer glows, or bevel effects to the logo.
  - ❌ Never detach or reposition the checkmark ribbon from the `x`.
