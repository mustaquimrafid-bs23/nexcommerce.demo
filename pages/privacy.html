THE DIRECTIVES

Five Pillars of Data Sovereignty

DIRECTIVE 01 Verified Covenant

Zero Data Brokerage Covenant

nexCommerce will never monetize, sell, lease, or broker client data to third-party ad networks, data brokers, or marketing syndicates. Your browsing intent, purchase history, and aesthetic affinities exist solely to power your private shopping experience.

“We reject surveillance advertising. Our commercial model is built entirely on the craftsmanship of our garments, timepieces, and acoustics.”
DIRECTIVE 02 Client-Side Vault

Biometric Sizing & Client Encryption

Anatomical sizing data collected via our Size & Fit Advisor or Style Profile is stored strictly within encrypted local browser vaults. When communicating sizing requirements to our master tailors, measurements are anonymized and associated only with a temporary commission token.

DIRECTIVE 03 PCI-DSS Level 1

Financial Tokenization & Ephemeral Sessions

All payment transactions through Klarna, iDEAL, Apple Pay, PayPal, Bancontact, SEPA, Visa, and Mastercard utilize direct cryptographic tokenization. Sensitive credentials bypass our web servers entirely and are routed through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant banking enclaves and PSD2 SCA protocols.

DIRECTIVE 04 Zero Fingerprinting

Telemetry Transparency & Local Storage Isolation

Every piece of stateful client data is inspectable in clear JSON format through the Local Data Sovereignty Controller above. We do not maintain hidden tracking cookies or cross-domain canvas fingerprints.

DIRECTIVE 05 100% Portability

Right to Complete Erasure & Portability

Clients maintain unconditional sovereignty to export their complete digital profile or execute an immediate 1-click purge of all local and server-side records at any time with zero retention latency.

STATUTORY SOVEREIGNTY

Your Rights Under GDPR & CCPA

You have the right to receive all personal data you have provided to us in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable JSON format. You can execute this immediately via the "Export Vault" button above.
You may erase all profile attributes, browsing cache, and stored preferences with one click. Triggering "Purge All" instantly clears your client records with zero retention latency.
nexCommerce allows you to freely disable or reset Smart silhouette recommendations at any time without degrading standard e-commerce catalog browsing or checkout capabilities.
You can modify your cookie categories, disable analytical telemetry, and clear saved cookie tokens at any time using the global Cookie Settings modal below.
