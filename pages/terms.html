Terms of Engagement & Consumer Rights

The legal covenant governing private client commissions, European consumer rights, statutory 14-day right of withdrawal, white-glove logistics, and cryptographic provenance verification.

Article 01

Orders

Every garment, acoustic instrument, and artisanal leather creation offered by nexCommerce is produced under strict small-batch craftsmanship standards. When a client places an order, the commission is recorded and scheduled across our partner ateliers in Milan, Paris, and Copenhagen.

"Because each piece is crafted with natural fibers, hand-finished edges, and tailored structural elements, minor nuances in grain, drape, and texture are hallmarks of genuine artisanal fabrication."

Bespoke commissions requiring individual proportion adaptations will be reviewed by our digital tailoring team prior to cutting. In the rare event of raw textile scarcity, clients will be notified within 24 hours with alternative artisan fabrics or immediate settlement refund.

Article 02

Prices & VAT

All transactions executed on this digital atelier are billed in Euros (EUR / €) inclusive of all statutory Value Added Tax (standard 19% German VAT or destination member state VAT rate) and European customs duties in accordance with the EU Price Indication Directive (Directive 98/6/EC & Directive (EU) 2019/2161). We offer real-time settlement tokenization through Klarna (Pay in 30 Days & Financing), iDEAL, Bancontact, Apple Pay, PayPal, SEPA Direct Debit, and Visa / Mastercard with mandatory 3D Secure 2 (3DS2) biometric verification conforming to EU PSD2 Regulatory Technical Standards (RTS).

Payment credentials are encrypted using bank-grade 256-bit AES encryption conforming to PCI-DSS Level 1 standards. At no point does nexCommerce store raw card numbers, CVCs, or bank authentication tokens on non-isolated infrastructure.

Article 03

Shipping & Delivery

From the moment an item clears final atelier inspection, it enters our secure handling process. Across the European Union, deliveries are executed exclusively via tracked DHL Express Carbon-Neutral and DPD European Ground services in tamper-evident FSC-certified sealed packaging.

"Clients receive real-time predictive GPS tracking and interactive delivery management via DHL On-Demand Delivery upon atelier dispatch."

Risk of loss transfers to the client only upon physical handover and signature confirmation. All shipments are 100% insured against loss or damage during transit at no additional cost to the client.

Article 04

14-Day Returns & Cancellations

In accordance with Directive 2011/83/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on Consumer Rights, you have the right to withdraw from this contract within fourteen (14) calendar days without giving any reason.

Withdrawal Period (Widerrufsfrist):

The withdrawal period will expire after 14 days from the day on which you acquire, or a third party other than the carrier and indicated by you acquires, physical possession of the goods.

To exercise the right of withdrawal, you must inform us (nexCommerce Atelier S.à r.l. & Co. KGaA, Friedrichstraße 43, 10117 Berlin, Germany, Phone: +49 (0) 30 8920 4100, E-Mail: concierge@nexcommerce.eu) of your decision to withdraw from this contract by an unequivocal statement (e.g. a letter sent by post, or an email). You may use the attached model withdrawal form, but it is not obligatory.

Effects of Withdrawal (Folgen des Widerrufs)

If you withdraw from this contract, we shall reimburse to you all payments received from you, including the costs of delivery (with the exception of supplementary costs resulting from your choice of a type of delivery other than the least expensive standard delivery offered by us), without undue delay and in any event not later than 14 days from the day on which we are informed about your decision to withdraw.

We will carry out such reimbursement using the same means of payment as you used for the initial transaction (e.g. direct refund to your original credit card, Klarna balance, or PayPal account). We may withhold reimbursement until we have received the goods back or you have supplied evidence of having sent back the goods.

We collect the goods complimentary from your residence via our prepaid DHL Express return courier. You are only liable for any diminished value of the goods resulting from handling other than what is necessary to establish the nature, characteristics, and functioning of the goods.

Article 06

Applicable European Law & Dispute Resolution

These Terms of Engagement are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Germany, excluding the UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG). If you are a consumer with habitual residence in another EU Member State, you also enjoy the protection of mandatory provisions of the law of your country of residence pursuant to Article 6(2) of the Rome I Regulation.

The European Commission provides a platform for online dispute resolution (ODR): https://ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr/.
