Terms of Engagement

The legal and ethical covenant governing private client commissions, white-glove fulfillment protocols, cryptographic provenance sovereignty, and complimentary atelier alterations.

Article 01

Atelier Craftsmanship & Commission Terms

Every garment, acoustic instrument, and artisanal leather creation offered by nexCommerce is produced under strict small-batch craftsmanship standards. When a client places an order, the commission is recorded and scheduled across our partner ateliers in Milan, Copenhagen, and Dhaka.

"Because each piece is crafted with natural fibers, hand-finished edges, and tailored structural elements, minor nuances in grain, drape, and texture are hallmarks of genuine artisanal fabrication."

Bespoke commissions requiring individual proportion adaptations will be reviewed by our digital tailoring team prior to cutting. In the rare event of raw textile scarcity, clients will be notified within 24 hours with alternative artisan fabrics or immediate settlement refund.

Article 02

Settlement & Sovereign Currency Standards

All transactions executed on this digital atelier are billed in Bangladeshi Taka (BDT) inclusive of all applicable luxury value-added taxes and atelier import duties. We offer real-time settlement tokenization through bKash, Nagad, Visa, and Mastercard.

Payment credentials are encrypted using bank-grade 256-bit AES encryption conforming to PCI-DSS Tier 1 standards. At no point does nexCommerce store raw card numbers, PINs, or OTPs on non-isolated infrastructure.

Article 03

White-Glove Fulfillment & Custody Protocols

From the moment an item clears final atelier inspection, it enters our white-glove custody protocol. In metropolitan Dhaka, deliveries are executed exclusively by our dedicated electric courier fleet in tamper-evident sealed packaging.

"Clients receive real-time sub-kilometer telemetry and live courier communication via our Order Journey portal upon dispatch."

Risk of loss transfers to the client only upon physical handover and biometric signature confirmation. All shipments are 100% insured against loss or damage during transit.

Article 04

Complimentary Alterations & Bespoke Return Policy

We stand unreservedly behind the anatomical proportion of our garments. If a piece requires minor structural refinement, clients are entitled to one complimentary alteration within 14 days of delivery through our Dhaka atelier network.

For standard ready-to-wear items, returns are accepted within 14 days of receipt provided the piece remains unworn, undamaged, with original security labels and cryptographic NFC tags intact. Returns are collected directly from your residence at no additional courier charge.

Article 05

Intellectual Property & Design Sovereignty

All silhouettes, acoustic acoustic driver geometries, typography, photographic lookbooks, and software architectures displayed on nexCommerce are the exclusive intellectual property of nexCommerce Atelier Inc. and protected by international copyright laws.

Unauthorized reproduction, commercial re-distribution, or programmatic scraping of catalog assets is strictly prohibited without explicit written covenant.

Article 06

Concierge Arbitration & Dispute Resolution

In the extraordinary event of a dispute or inquiry regarding a commission, our Private Client Legal Concierge is empowered to resolve matters through binding mediation before formal jurisdictional proceedings.

These Terms of Engagement are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Bangladesh, without regard to conflict of law principles.
