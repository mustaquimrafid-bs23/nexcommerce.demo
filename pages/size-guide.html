PRECISION ANATOMICAL METRICS

How to Measure

01
Chest Circumference

Measure horizontally around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape comfortable under the arms.

02
Shoulder Breadth

Measure across the upper back from the tip of one acromion bone to the tip of the other.

03
Natural Waistline

Measure around the narrowest section of your natural waist, roughly 2 inches above the navel.

04
Foot Length & Insole

Place your heel against a flat wall on paper, mark your longest toe, and measure straight along the axis.
